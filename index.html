ESPU AI Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

ESPU AI Launcher operates on your Android device without an ESPU account. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, subscription system, or remote customer-data service.

Data used on the device

ESPU reads apps that expose launcher activities so it can work as a home-screen replacement. It stores launcher preferences—such as your optional Home greeting name, favorites, recents, hidden choices, focus choices, freeform positions, themes, and quick notes—in the app's private local storage.

If you explicitly grant Notification Access, ESPU can display active notification titles and message previews in the ESPU Island and in the relevant app's long-press preview. ESPU can offer a reply when the source app provides Android's standard quick-reply action. ESPU processes this information on the device and does not upload it. Notification content is not persisted by default; only when you explicitly choose Save for follow-up does ESPU copy that visible notification's title and text into the app-private Intent Inbox. Hidden notification content is never saved. Repeating charging, USB, battery, System UI, and persistent service notices are filtered from ESPU surfaces and remain Android's responsibility. If you choose a per-app Island snooze, ESPU stores only that app's package name and the snooze expiry in private local storage, with a maximum of 256 active entries.

If you explicitly grant Contacts access and ask ESPU AI to call or text a saved person, ESPU searches contact names and phone numbers on the device to prepare that request. ESPU does not persist or upload contact records and refuses ambiguous contact matches.

If you explicitly ask ESPU AI to call one exact person or ordinary phone number, ESPU first shows the exact contact and normalized number in a visible per-call review. Choosing Cancel or dismissing that review opens no call or dialer. Choosing Call may then trigger ESPU's separate just-in-time explanation and Android's Phone-permission prompt. Carrier/USSD strings and pause/wait dialing codes are rejected. Emergency numbers, an uncertain platform emergency verdict, uncertain modem routing, or a missing default-Phone role use the protected ACTION_DIAL surface so Android's dialer retains final confirmation and emergency-routing authority. An ordinary direct call can be placed only after Android verifies that ESPU holds the default-Phone role and grants CALL_PHONE; ESPU then asks Android Telecom to place that validated number through TelecomManager.placeCall. Permission denial or cancelling the permission explanation opens nothing. ESPU does not call from search results, suggestions, recommendations, or ambiguous contacts, and this direct-call path does not read or write call history.

If you separately choose ESPU as Android's default Phone app, Android Telecom provides ESPU with the active call state, caller display information, supported call capabilities, and available audio endpoints needed to present incoming and ongoing call controls. Verified ESPU Island controls can include Answer, Decline, End, Mute, Android-provided audio routes such as available Bluetooth devices, Keypad, and Hold when the active call supports it. If another Phone app remains selected, ESPU disables incoming and ongoing call cards and call actions instead of deriving call ownership from that app's notification; ordinary notification, message, media, voicemail, and compatible live-activity Island surfaces remain available. Android Telecom and system safeguards retain protected emergency-call routing.

While ESPU remains the default Phone app, you may separately grant read-only Call history and Contacts permissions to show Recents and local contacts inside ESPU Phone and start a call you select. ESPU stops querying these sources when it is no longer the default Phone app or the permission is revoked. If you long-press one recent call or choose Clear all, ESPU shows a confirmation first. Only after confirmation, while ESPU is still the default Phone app, may ESPU request Android's Call history change permission and delete the selected record or all records from Android's on-device call history. ESPU does not otherwise edit call history. Call history and contacts are processed on your device. ESPU does not record or upload call audio, create a cloud call history, upload contacts, or use call information for advertising or analytics.

ESPU does not request Phone or Phone state access during first-run setup. If you open ESPU Phone and ask to choose an outgoing SIM or eSIM, ESPU first shows a separate explanation and may then request Android's read-only Phone state permission. That access is used only to list Android-provided enabled call-capable accounts and route your outgoing call through the account you select. ESPU does not read or store your phone number, SIM serial, ICCID, call content, or a raw Android account handle. If you decline, Android can still choose the calling account. A one-way local digest may remember the selection for matching only on your device and is cleared when the account disappears. ESPU does not request ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS. Answer, Decline, Mute, audio route, Keypad, Hold, and End use Android Telecom only while ESPU holds the default-Phone role.

If you enable screen-lock access, Android grants ESPU only the device-admin force-lock policy. ESPU invokes it only after your double-tap gesture or an ESPU AI voice command that you confirm on screen. ESPU does not erase the device, change its screen-lock credential, restart or power off the device, or administer other policies.

Private Space uses Android's system authentication prompt. ESPU does not receive or store fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or password data. Hidden choices affect ESPU's interface; they do not uninstall or encrypt an app.

ESPU does not request hidden-profile access and does not expose or control Android's system Private Space. If Android nevertheless reports an app as belonging to a private profile, ESPU excludes it from ordinary All Applications. This is separate from ESPU's own hidden-app Private Space described above.

Battery/charging status, power-saver state, local time zone, and Android wallpaper colors may be read locally to adapt the interface. ESPU does not request location access.

ESPU AI processes typing and explicit voice requests locally through its compact Action Model and bounded deterministic command handlers. Each supported action still uses the same permission, role, confirmation, current-state, and result checks at its execution boundary. ESPU can open installed apps and supported Android destinations, start timers, open alarms and calendar views, prepare communication or calendar drafts for review, control an active media session, answer limited device-status questions, and change supported ESPU launcher settings. “Deep Thinking” is deterministic on-device planning and comparison logic; it is not a remote generative-AI service and does not claim live facts.

ESPU AI includes ESPU's own compact Action Model in the signed app. It recognizes supported requests and proposes typed actions, but it has no Android privileges. Reviewed code still enforces device capability, profile privacy, roles, permissions, confirmations, and result checks. It is an action model, not an open-ended language model, and it does not claim cached facts are current.

Optional automatic action learning is off until you enable it. When enabled, a bounded local adapter learns hashed feature weights only from verified low-risk outcomes. You may separately teach a private phrase only by linking it to one supported low-risk open action; ambiguous, sensitive, external, contact, message, search, and navigation examples are rejected. ESPU does not keep raw commands, contact names, phone numbers, message text, or document contents as action-model training examples. You can disable, inspect, forget, reset, or erase that learning. Android Clear storage or a full uninstall removes it. No future document, vision, language, cross-device, app-skill, enterprise, or OEM provider is active in ESPU. Any later provider would require separate signed delivery, review, compatibility, consent, and real device authority.

Suggested Apps & Coach learning is a separate opt-in control. Everyday Apps remains the default Home dock. When enabled, ESPU AI stores bounded app-launch counters, weekday/weekend time contexts, recent-use timestamps, next-app counters, and suggestion feedback in private local storage. Turning it off pauses new habit collection and hides its suggestions. ESPU does not retain a raw activity timeline. Suggested Apps stays unavailable until ESPU records at least 3 successful launcher opens across at least 2 local days, and appears only after you explicitly select Suggested; ESPU never opens the suggested app by itself. Coach setting changes require your approval. While Private Space is locked, ESPU AI filters hidden apps from notification results.

ESPU Notes stores plain notes, checklists, pin choices, and the compatible Quick Note in app-private local storage. ESPU has no email-account connector or automatic mailbox sync. If you explicitly choose Android's Share or Process Text action and select ESPU, ESPU stores only that selected payload as a local Intent Inbox draft. Manual drafts and notification content explicitly saved for follow-up use the same bounded local store. You can review or delete each draft. Converting a draft to a note happens locally; reminder, calendar, call, or message actions open an Android-owned review surface and are not treated as complete merely because that surface opened. ESPU does not continuously read another app or mailbox.

ESPU is an Android Home app with an optional default-Phone mode, not an operating system. Android owns protected system UI, emergency routing, car-display and Android Auto surfaces, permission prompts, package installation, and package removal. ESPU does not have unrestricted access to your phone. Android roles, permissions, installed-app support, confirmation screens, and protected-system limits determine which actions can run. ESPU does not bypass Android security, silently send messages, place unsolicited calls, save calendar entries without review, install apps, change protected settings, or read another app's private data. A direct call occurs only after an explicit call command, exact-target validation, a visible per-call target review, the in-context permission disclosure when needed, verified ESPU default-Phone role ownership, and Android's CALL_PHONE permission.

ESPU AI does not access, read, or store passwords, PINs, banking details, or payment credentials. Never enter them in an ESPU AI command.

If you enable “Hey Espu” anywhere, ESPU uses microphone access in a user-visible Android foreground service. ESPU prefers on-device recognition and includes an offline Vosk fallback in the applicable build. Depending on device support and configuration, Android's configured speech provider may process a spoken command under that provider's privacy terms. ESPU has no ESPU voice-upload server. Android shows a persistent microphone notification and you can turn listening off at any time.

Home weather ambience can use the device clock for automatic scenes. If you enable live weather, ESPU stores the selected place, resolved coordinates, and latest conditions in private local storage so the ambience can be restored. ESPU sends the city or postal code you enter over HTTPS to Open-Meteo's geocoding service, then sends the resolved latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo's forecast service. ESPU does not request Android device-location permission for this feature. Turning off live sync clears the saved live-weather profile.

If you choose My Photo, Android's system document picker grants ESPU persistent read access only to the image you selected. ESPU stores the selected content reference and a locally sampled average color, then uses Android's WallpaperManager to apply the image to the system Home wallpaper and, when you explicitly choose it on a supported device, the Lock wallpaper. ESPU does not request gallery or broad file access or upload the photo. Android Auto and other car-display backgrounds remain independently controlled by Android and the vehicle.

ESPU listens for Android package-added and package-removed events so newly installed launcher apps can appear without a manual reload and confirmed removals can disappear. Choosing Uninstall opens Android's required confirmation screen; ESPU refreshes its local launcher data only after Android confirms the package is gone.

Android widgets remain provided by their source apps. ESPU shows one compatible widget choice per visible installed app and stores Android-assigned widget identifiers locally so it can host up to four independent Home widget cards. Removing a widget releases its identifier but does not uninstall its provider app.

Sharing and backup

ESPU does not automatically share personal data. If you choose Export Backup, ESPU creates a JSON file of launcher preferences and hands it to Android's system share/document interface. You choose the destination. Backup files can contain app package names and hidden/focus choices.

Retention and deletion

Launcher data remains on the device until you change it, restore a backup, clear ESPU storage, or uninstall ESPU. Notes and Intent Inbox drafts remain until you delete them, clear ESPU storage, or uninstall ESPU. ESPU AI settings let you inspect or forget Action Model learning, reset learned and taught matching signals, separately reset Suggested Apps & Coach learning, or erase all ESPU AI-owned state without removing your launcher layout or apps. Notification content is not copied by default; a draft copy exists only after Save for follow-up. Revoking Notification Access stops ESPU from reading active notifications but does not silently delete drafts you previously chose to save. ESPU does not create a user account.

Network access

Direct device commands, local ideas, and opt-in bounded learning have no ESPU AI-server or remote generative-model integration. A configured Android speech provider may process spoken commands as described above. ESPU does not send learned app patterns, installed apps, contacts, notifications, Home layout, launcher data, command history, or a device identifier to an AI provider. The ESPU-owned action model is part of the signed app; optional future capability packs remain unavailable until separately signed, reviewed, consented, and tested.

Live weather uses the configured HTTPS weather provider.
