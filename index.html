ESPU AI Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

ESPU AI Launcher operates on your Android device without an ESPU account. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, subscription system, or remote customer-data service.

Data used on the device

ESPU reads apps that expose launcher activities so it can work as a home-screen replacement. It stores launcher preferences—such as your optional Home greeting name, favorites, recents, hidden choices, focus choices, freeform positions, themes, and quick notes—in the app's private local storage.

If you explicitly grant Notification Access, ESPU can display active notification titles and message previews in the ESPU Island and in the relevant app's long-press preview. ESPU can offer a reply when the source app provides Android's standard quick-reply action. ESPU processes this information on the device, does not create a separate notification inbox, and does not upload or persist notification content. Repeating charging, USB, battery, System UI, and persistent service notices are filtered from ESPU surfaces and remain Android's responsibility. If you choose a per-app Island snooze, ESPU stores only that app's package name and the snooze expiry in private local storage, with a maximum of 256 active entries.

If you explicitly grant Contacts access and ask ESPU AI to call or text a saved person, ESPU searches contact names and phone numbers on the device to prepare that request. ESPU does not persist or upload contact records and refuses ambiguous contact matches.

If you explicitly ask ESPU AI to call one exact person or ordinary phone number, ESPU separately explains and requests Android's Phone permission just in time. When granted, ESPU may submit that one number to Android with ACTION_CALL. ESPU does not call from search results, suggestions, recommendations, or ambiguous contacts, and this direct-call path does not read or write call history. Carrier/USSD strings and pause/wait dialing codes are rejected. If permission is declined, direct calling is unavailable, or Android identifies—or cannot safely rule out—an emergency number, ESPU uses ACTION_DIAL so Android's dialer retains confirmation and emergency-routing authority.

If you separately choose ESPU as Android's default Phone app, Android Telecom provides ESPU with the active call state, caller display information, supported call capabilities, and available audio endpoints needed to present incoming and ongoing call controls. Verified ESPU Island controls can include Answer, Decline, End, Mute, Android-provided audio routes such as available Bluetooth devices, Keypad, and Hold when the active call supports it. Keeping another Phone app limits ESPU to controls Android or that app exposes. Android Telecom and system safeguards retain protected emergency-call routing.

While ESPU remains the default Phone app, you may separately grant read-only Call history and Contacts permissions to show Recents and local contacts inside ESPU Phone and start a call you select. ESPU stops querying these sources when it is no longer the default Phone app or the permission is revoked. If you long-press one recent call or choose Clear all, ESPU shows a confirmation first. Only after confirmation, while ESPU is still the default Phone app, may ESPU request Android's Call history change permission and delete the selected record or all records from Android's on-device call history. ESPU does not otherwise edit call history. Call history and contacts are processed on your device. ESPU does not record or upload call audio, create a cloud call history, upload contacts, or use call information for advertising or analytics.

If you enable screen-lock access, Android grants ESPU only the device-admin force-lock policy. ESPU invokes it only after your double-tap gesture or an ESPU AI voice command that you confirm on screen. ESPU does not erase the device, change its screen-lock credential, restart or power off the device, or administer other policies.

Private Space uses Android's system authentication prompt. ESPU does not receive or store fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or password data. Hidden choices affect ESPU's interface; they do not uninstall or encrypt an app.

ESPU does not request hidden-profile access and does not expose or control Android's system Private Space. If Android nevertheless reports an app as belonging to a private profile, ESPU excludes it from ordinary All Applications. This is separate from ESPU's own hidden-app Private Space described above.

Battery/charging status, power-saver state, local time zone, and Android wallpaper colors may be read locally to adapt the interface. ESPU does not request location access.

ESPU AI uses one local command path for typing, explicit voice requests, and Home recommendations. It can open installed apps and supported Android destinations, start timers, open alarms and calendar views, prepare communication or calendar drafts for review, control an active media session, answer limited device-status questions, and change ESPU launcher settings. “Deep Thinking” is deterministic on-device planning and comparison logic; it is not a remote generative-AI service and does not claim live facts.

Private Intelligence is an optional, build- and device-dependent ESPU AI mode designed to use ESPU's curated Gemma 3 270M model on the device. ESPU enables it only after you review and accept the displayed Gemma terms and use restrictions and ESPU completes Install, exact model verification, and a local self-test. Installation alone is not a readiness claim; the model can answer only when ESPU shows Ready on this phone. Depending on the distribution build, the package may be included in ESPU or obtained on demand through Google Play after you start setup. Availability, redistribution approval, Play delivery, and customer-device readiness must each be established for the final release and are not implied by this policy.

When Private Intelligence is active, its prompts and generated responses are processed on your device. ESPU does not send them to an ESPU server or a remote model provider. The model package, terms-acceptance record, and activation state remain in ESPU's private app storage. Remove model deletes the activated model package and state; Android Clear storage or a full uninstall removes ESPU's private model data. Deterministic launcher commands continue to work without the model.

When you enable Private adaptive learning, ESPU AI stores bounded app-launch counters, weekday/weekend time contexts, recent-use timestamps, next-app counters, and suggestion feedback in private local storage. Turning it off pauses new habit collection. ESPU does not retain a raw activity timeline. App-habit suggestions require repeated, recent on-device evidence and ESPU asks before applying every change. While Private Space is locked, ESPU AI filters hidden apps from notification results.

ESPU is an Android Home app with an optional default-Phone mode, not an operating system. Android owns protected system UI, emergency routing, car-display and Android Auto surfaces, permission prompts, package installation, and package removal. ESPU does not have unrestricted access to your phone. Android roles, permissions, installed-app support, confirmation screens, and protected-system limits determine which actions can run. ESPU does not bypass Android security, silently send messages, place unsolicited calls, save calendar entries without review, install apps, change protected settings, or read another app's private data. A direct call occurs only after an explicit call command, exact-target validation, the in-context disclosure, and the applicable Android Phone authorization.

ESPU AI does not access, read, or store passwords, PINs, banking details, or payment credentials. Never enter them in an ESPU AI command.

If you enable “Hey Espu” anywhere, ESPU uses microphone access in a user-visible Android foreground service. ESPU prefers on-device recognition and includes an offline Vosk fallback in the applicable build. Depending on device support and configuration, Android's configured speech provider may process a spoken command under that provider's privacy terms. ESPU has no ESPU voice-upload server. Android shows a persistent microphone notification and you can turn listening off at any time.

Home weather ambience can use the device clock for automatic scenes. If you enable live weather, ESPU stores the selected place, resolved coordinates, and latest conditions in private local storage so the ambience can be restored. ESPU sends the city or postal code you enter over HTTPS to Open-Meteo's geocoding service, then sends the resolved latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo's forecast service. ESPU does not request Android device-location permission for this feature. Turning off live sync clears the saved live-weather profile.

If you choose My Photo, Android's system document picker grants ESPU persistent read access only to the image you selected. ESPU stores the selected content reference and a locally sampled average color, then uses Android's WallpaperManager to apply the image to the system Home wallpaper and, when you explicitly choose it on a supported device, the Lock wallpaper. ESPU does not request gallery or broad file access or upload the photo. Android Auto and other car-display backgrounds remain independently controlled by Android and the vehicle.

ESPU listens for Android package-added and package-removed events so newly installed launcher apps can appear without a manual reload and confirmed removals can disappear. Choosing Uninstall opens Android's required confirmation screen; ESPU refreshes its local launcher data only after Android confirms the package is gone.

Android widgets remain provided by their source apps. ESPU shows one compatible widget choice per visible installed app and stores Android-assigned widget identifiers locally so it can host five Home widget slots. Removing a widget releases its identifier but does not uninstall its provider app.

Sharing and backup

ESPU does not automatically share personal data. If you choose Export Backup, ESPU creates a JSON file of launcher preferences and hands it to Android's system share/document interface. You choose the destination. Backup files can contain app package names and hidden/focus choices.

Retention and deletion

Launcher data remains on the device until you change it, restore a backup, clear ESPU storage, or uninstall ESPU. ESPU AI settings let you inspect aggregate learning totals and erase only learned habits and Coach feedback without resetting your launcher layout or notes. Notification content is not copied into ESPU's persistent storage; per-app Island snooze expiries are preferences and can be cleared with ESPU storage or uninstall. Revoking Notification Access stops ESPU from reading active notifications. ESPU does not create a user account.

Network access

Direct device commands, local ideas, adaptive learning, and Private Intelligence have no ESPU AI-server or remote generative-model integration. A configured Android speech provider may process spoken commands as described above. ESPU does not send learned app patterns, installed apps, contacts, notifications, Home layout, launcher data, Private Intelligence prompts, or a device identifier to an AI provider. A Play-distributed build may use Google Play's on-demand asset-delivery infrastructure when you choose to install or update the optional Private Intelligence model.
