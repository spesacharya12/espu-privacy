ESPU AI Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This policy applies to ESPU AI Launcher 1.6 (version code 11).

ESPU AI Launcher operates on your Android device without an ESPU account. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, subscription system, or remote customer-data service.

Data used on the device

ESPU reads apps that expose launcher activities so it can work as a home-screen replacement. It stores launcher preferences—such as your optional Home greeting name, favorites, recents, hidden choices, focus choices, freeform positions, icon choices, themes, morning/day/evening/night appearance schedules, and quick notes—in the app's private local storage.

If you explicitly grant Notification Access, ESPU can display currently active notification titles and message previews in the ESPU Island and in the relevant app's long-press preview. ESPU can group active notices and invoke only actions the source app exposes through Android, such as quick reply and, for supported call notifications, answer, decline, mute, speaker, or end controls. Action availability and final handling remain under Android and the source Phone or communication app; ESPU does not create its own Telecom actions. ESPU processes notification information on the device, maintains only the current active snapshot, does not create a historical notification inbox, and does not upload or persist notification content. Repeating charging, USB, battery, System UI, and persistent service notices are filtered from ESPU surfaces and remain Android's responsibility. If you choose a per-app Island snooze, ESPU stores only that app's package name and the snooze expiry in private local storage, with a maximum of 256 active entries.

If you explicitly grant Contacts access and ask ESPU AI to call or text a saved person, ESPU searches contact names and phone numbers on the device to prepare that request. ESPU does not persist or upload contact records and refuses ambiguous contact matches.

If you explicitly ask ESPU AI to call one exact person or ordinary phone number, ESPU separately explains and requests Android's Phone permission just in time. When granted, ESPU may submit that one number to Android with ACTION_CALL. ESPU does not call from search results, suggestions, recommendations, or ambiguous contacts, and it does not read or write the call log. Carrier/USSD strings and pause/wait dialing codes are rejected. If permission is declined, direct calling is unavailable, or Android identifies—or cannot safely rule out—an emergency number, ESPU uses ACTION_DIAL so Android's dialer retains confirmation and emergency-routing authority.

If you enable screen-lock access, Android grants ESPU only the device-admin force-lock policy. ESPU invokes it only after your double-tap gesture or an ESPU AI voice command that you confirm on screen. ESPU does not erase the device, change its screen-lock credential, restart or power off the device, or administer other policies.

Private Space uses Android's system authentication prompt. ESPU does not receive or store fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or password data. Hidden choices affect ESPU's interface; they do not uninstall or encrypt an app.

ESPU does not request hidden-profile access and does not expose or control Android's system Private Space. If Android nevertheless reports an app as belonging to a private profile, ESPU excludes it from ordinary All Applications. This is separate from ESPU's own hidden-app Private Space described above.

Battery/charging status, power-saver state, local time zone, and Android wallpaper colors may be read locally to adapt the interface. ESPU does not request location access.

ESPU AI uses one local command path for typing, explicit voice requests, and Home recommendations. It can open installed apps and supported Android destinations, start timers, open alarms and calendar views, prepare communication or calendar drafts for review, control an active media session, answer limited device-status questions, and change ESPU launcher settings. “Deep Thinking” is deterministic on-device planning and comparison logic; it is not a remote generative-AI service and does not claim live facts.

Private Intelligence is an optional, device-dependent ESPU AI mode that uses ESPU's curated Gemma 3 270M model on the device. The ESPU 1.6 Google Play bundle includes the model package, so setup does not send you to a model website or require a technical file import. ESPU activates the model only after you review and accept the displayed Gemma terms and use restrictions and ESPU completes installation into app-private storage, exact size and SHA-256 verification, and a local self-test. Installation alone is not a readiness claim; the model can answer only when ESPU shows Ready on this phone.

When Private Intelligence is active, its prompts and generated responses are processed on your device. ESPU does not send them to an ESPU server or a remote model provider. The activated model copy, terms-acceptance record, verification digest, and activation state remain in ESPU's private app storage. Remove model deletes that activated copy and its state; the model distributed inside the installed ESPU package remains available for setup again. Android Clear storage removes ESPU's activated private copy and state, and a full uninstall removes both ESPU's installed package and its private model data. Deterministic launcher commands continue to work without the model.

When you enable Private adaptive learning, ESPU AI stores bounded app-launch counters, weekday/weekend time contexts, recent-use timestamps, next-app counters, and suggestion feedback in private local storage. Turning it off pauses new habit collection. ESPU does not retain a raw activity timeline. App-habit suggestions require repeated, recent on-device evidence and ESPU asks before applying every change. While Private Space is locked, ESPU AI filters hidden apps from notification results.

ESPU is an Android Home app, not an operating system. Android owns protected system UI, car-display and Android Auto surfaces, permission prompts, package installation, and package removal. ESPU does not have unrestricted access to your phone. Android permissions, installed-app support, confirmation screens, and protected-system limits determine which actions can run. ESPU does not bypass Android security, silently send messages, place unsolicited calls, save calendar entries without review, install apps, change protected settings, or read another app's private data. A direct call occurs only after an explicit call command, exact-target validation, the in-context disclosure, and Android's Phone permission.

ESPU AI does not access, read, or store passwords, PINs, banking details, or payment credentials. Never enter them in an ESPU AI command.

If you enable “Hey Espu” anywhere, ESPU uses microphone access in a user-visible Android foreground service. ESPU prefers on-device recognition and includes an offline Vosk fallback in the applicable build. Depending on device support and configuration, Android's configured speech provider may process a spoken command under that provider's privacy terms. ESPU has no ESPU voice-upload server. Android shows a persistent microphone notification and you can turn listening off at any time.

Home weather ambience can use the device clock for automatic scenes. If you enable live weather, ESPU stores the selected place, resolved coordinates, and latest conditions in private local storage so the ambience can be restored. ESPU sends the city or postal code you enter over HTTPS to Open-Meteo's geocoding service, then sends the resolved latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo's forecast service. ESPU does not request Android device-location permission for this feature. Turning off live sync clears the saved live-weather profile.

If you choose My Photo, Android's system document picker grants ESPU persistent read access only to the image you selected. ESPU stores the selected content reference and a locally sampled average color, then uses Android's WallpaperManager to apply the image to the system Home wallpaper and, when you explicitly choose it on a supported device, the Lock wallpaper. ESPU does not request gallery or broad file access or upload the photo. Android Auto and other car-display backgrounds remain independently controlled by Android and the vehicle.

ESPU listens for Android package-added and package-removed events so newly installed launcher apps can appear without a manual reload and confirmed removals can disappear. Choosing Uninstall opens Android's required confirmation screen; ESPU refreshes its local launcher data only after Android confirms the package is gone.

Android widgets remain provided by their source apps. ESPU shows one compatible widget choice per visible installed app and stores Android-assigned widget identifiers locally so it can host five Home widget slots. Removing a widget releases its identifier but does not uninstall its provider app.

Sharing and backup

ESPU does not automatically share personal data. If you choose Export Backup, ESPU creates a JSON file of launcher preferences and hands it to Android's system share/document interface. You choose the destination. Backup files can contain app package names and hidden/focus choices.

If you choose Share private support report, ESPU creates a text report containing the ESPU and Android versions, a broad device-size class, enabled-capability and readiness states, non-personal layout and theme settings, and up to 12 allow-listed error surface/code pairs from the previous 14 days. The report excludes app lists, personal names, contacts, notification content, searches, prompts, location, device identifiers, learned habits, and raw error text. ESPU hands the report to Android's system share interface only after you request it, and you choose the destination.

Retention and deletion

Launcher data remains on the device until you change it, restore a backup, clear ESPU storage, or uninstall ESPU. ESPU AI settings let you inspect aggregate learning totals and erase only learned habits and Coach feedback without resetting your launcher layout or notes. Notification content is not copied into ESPU's persistent storage; per-app Island snooze expiries are preferences and can be cleared with ESPU storage or uninstall. The sanitized support-error ledger is limited to 12 allow-listed entries, ignores entries older than 14 days when a report is created, and is removed by clearing ESPU storage or uninstalling the app. Revoking Notification Access stops ESPU from reading active notifications. ESPU does not create a user account.

Network access

Direct device commands, local ideas, adaptive learning, and Private Intelligence have no ESPU AI-server or remote generative-model integration. A configured Android speech provider may process spoken commands as described above. ESPU does not send learned app patterns, installed apps, contacts, notifications, Home layout, launcher data, Private Intelligence prompts, or a device identifier to an AI provider. Google Play delivers the ESPU 1.6 model package with the app; Private Intelligence setup itself does not contact Hugging Face, another model website, or an ESPU AI server.
