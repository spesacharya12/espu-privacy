ESPU AI Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

ESPU AI Launcher operates on your Android device without an ESPU account. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, subscription system, or remote customer-data service.

Data used on the device

ESPU reads apps that expose launcher activities so it can work as a home-screen replacement. It stores launcher preferences—such as your optional Home greeting name, favorites, recents, hidden choices, focus choices, freeform positions, themes, and quick notes—in the app's private local storage.

If you explicitly grant Notification Access, ESPU can display active notification titles and message previews in the ESPU Island and in the relevant app's long-press preview. ESPU can offer a reply when the source app provides Android's standard quick-reply action. Notification text is processed transiently on the device. A small publisher-artwork image may be held in a bounded local cache only while its notification remains active; it is deleted when that notification ends or listener access disconnects. ESPU does not create or upload a notification archive. Private Glance replaces locked private content with fixed redacted text and exposes no publisher actions. Repeating charging, USB, battery, System UI, and persistent service notices are filtered from ESPU surfaces and remain Android's responsibility.

Text received through Android Share or Process Text stays only in the current ESPU AI composer. ESPU does not add that shared text to its retained local context memory.

If you explicitly grant Contacts access and ask ESPU AI to call or text a saved person, ESPU searches contact names and phone numbers on the device to prepare the requested Android dialer or message draft. ESPU does not persist or upload contact records.

If you enable screen-lock access, Android grants ESPU only the device-admin force-lock policy. ESPU invokes it from Lock Now, your configured Home double-tap gesture, or an ESPU AI voice lock command that you confirm. ESPU does not erase the device, change its screen-lock credential, restart or power off the device, or administer other policies.

Private Space uses Android's system authentication prompt. ESPU does not receive or store fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or password data. Hidden choices affect ESPU's interface; they do not uninstall or encrypt an app.

Battery/charging status, power-saver state, local time zone, and Android wallpaper colors may be read locally to adapt the interface. If you enable Adaptive Light Grading, ESPU samples the ambient-light sensor only while the launcher is visible, keeps no reading history, and does not use the camera or location. ESPU does not request location access.

Your ESPU display-language preference stays on the device. The current verified packs are English, Nepali, Hindi, and Spanish; System default selects a supported matching pack and otherwise falls back to English. ESPU sends no interface text to a translation service.

ESPU AI uses one local command path for typing, explicit voice requests, and Home recommendations. It can open installed apps and supported Android destinations, start timers, open alarms and calendar views, prepare communication or calendar drafts for review, control an active media session, answer limited device-status questions, and change ESPU launcher settings. “Deep Thinking” is deterministic on-device planning and comparison logic; it is not a remote generative-AI service and does not claim live facts.

When you enable Private adaptive learning, ESPU AI stores bounded app-launch counters, weekday/weekend time contexts, recent-use timestamps, next-app counters, and suggestion feedback in private local storage. Turning it off pauses new habit collection. ESPU does not retain a raw activity timeline. App-habit suggestions require repeated, recent on-device evidence and ESPU asks before applying every change. While Private Space is locked, ESPU AI filters hidden apps from notification results.

ESPU is an Android Home app, not an operating system. Android owns protected system UI, car-display and Android Auto surfaces, permission prompts, package installation, and package removal. ESPU does not have unrestricted access to your phone. Android permissions, installed-app support, confirmation screens, and protected-system limits determine which actions can run. ESPU does not bypass Android security, silently send messages, place calls, save calendar entries, install apps, change protected settings, or read another app's private data.

ESPU AI does not access, read, or store passwords, PINs, banking details, or payment credentials. Never enter them in an ESPU AI command.

If you enable “Hey Espu” anywhere, ESPU uses microphone access in a user-visible Android foreground service and tries the phone's configured speech recognition first. The private Vosk Offline English fallback is not bundled in the base install: it is a separate optional, on-demand Google Play download and is used only when the Android speech provider remains unavailable. Direct-install builds continue to support Android speech and tap-to-talk without that pack. A configured Android speech provider may process a command under that provider's privacy terms. ESPU does not send voice recordings to an ESPU server. Android shows a persistent microphone notification and you can turn listening off at any time.

Home weather ambience can use the device clock for automatic scenes or retrieve weather for a place you explicitly enter. A live-weather request sends that place query to the weather/geocoding service but does not use Android device-location permission.

If you choose My Photo, Android's system document picker grants ESPU persistent read access only to the image you selected. ESPU stores the selected content reference and a locally sampled average color, then uses Android's WallpaperManager to apply the image to the system Home wallpaper and, when you explicitly choose it on a supported device, the Lock wallpaper. ESPU does not request gallery or broad file access or upload the photo. Android Auto and other car-display backgrounds remain independently controlled by Android and the vehicle.

Optional original wallpaper and Driving collections are delivered on demand by Google Play. A selected Driving design can be applied to supported phone Home and optional Lock wallpaper destinations. ESPU can open Android Auto Settings, but it cannot set the Android Auto host wallpaper.

Charging Display can show a seven-day calendar derived only from the phone's date, locale, and time zone. ESPU requests no calendar permission and reads no private calendar events. ESPU Lock Glance is a clock, date, and ambience AppWidget for Android devices that support keyguard widgets; it does not replace or bypass the secure lock screen.

ESPU listens for Android package-added and package-removed events so newly installed launcher apps can appear without a manual reload and confirmed removals can disappear. Choosing Uninstall opens Android's required confirmation screen; ESPU refreshes its local launcher data only after Android confirms the package is gone.

Android widgets remain provided by their source apps. ESPU shows one compatible widget choice per visible installed app and stores Android-assigned widget identifiers locally so it can host five Home widget slots. Removing a widget releases its identifier but does not uninstall its provider app.

Sharing and backup

ESPU does not automatically share personal data. If you choose Export Backup, ESPU creates a JSON file from an explicit launcher-preference allowlist and hands it to Android's system share/document interface. You choose the destination. ESPU removes hidden-app identifiers and every nested reference to a protected package from Favorites, ordering, focus, freeform, App Cover, and portable Icon Power data before export. Device-bound widget IDs, notifications, notes, voice data, weather place, greeting name, permissions, and Private Space membership are excluded.

Retention and deletion

Launcher data remains on the device until you change it, restore a backup, clear ESPU storage, or uninstall ESPU. ESPU AI settings let you inspect aggregate learning totals and erase only learned habits and Coach feedback without resetting your launcher layout or notes. Revoking Notification Access stops ESPU from reading active notifications. ESPU does not create a user account.

Network access

Direct device commands, wake-word recognition, local ideas, and adaptive learning have no AI-server or AI-provider integration. ESPU does not send learned app patterns, installed apps, contacts, notifications, Home layout, launcher data, or a device identifier to an AI provider.
