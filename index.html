ESPU AI Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

ESPU AI Launcher operates on your Android device without an ESPU account. The app contains no advertising SDK, ESPU-operated analytics service, subscription system, or remote customer-data service. The bundled Google ML Kit text-recognition SDK used only after an explicit image/PDF selection may transmit SDK diagnostics and usage measurements described below; it does not send the selected image, recognized text, or recognition result to Google.

This policy describes the ESPU 2.3, version code 18 closed-testing candidate. It documents that candidate's behavior only; it is not production approval, production release authorization, or a claim that Google Play Production review is complete.

Data used on the device

ESPU reads apps that expose launcher activities so it can work as a home-screen replacement. It stores launcher preferences—such as your optional Home greeting name, favorites, recents, hidden choices, focus choices, freeform positions, themes, and quick notes—in the app's private local storage.

If you explicitly grant Notification Access, ESPU can display active notification titles and message previews in the ESPU Island and in the relevant app's long-press preview. ESPU can offer a reply when the source app provides Android's standard quick-reply action. ESPU processes this information on the device and does not upload it. Notification content is not persisted by default; only when you explicitly choose Save for follow-up does ESPU copy that visible notification's title and text into the app-private Intent Inbox. Hidden notification content is never saved. Repeating charging, USB, battery, System UI, and persistent service notices are filtered from ESPU surfaces and remain Android's responsibility. If you choose a per-app Island snooze, ESPU stores only that app's package name and the snooze expiry in private local storage, with a maximum of 256 active entries.

If you explicitly grant Contacts access, that permission supports contact-aware onboarding, ESPU Phone and caller names, ESPU AI call or text requests, and optional Contacts results in Universal Search. ESPU reads contact display names, phone numbers, phone labels, and lookup keys on your device and refuses ambiguous contact matches. On Android 12 or later, a bounded copy of an eligible contact's display name, number, label, and lookup key is stored in app-private AppSearch solely for on-device Universal Search. ESPU does not upload contact data. If Contacts permission is revoked, ESPU purges contact documents after the revocation is detected during an eligible search query or index rebuild. Clearing ESPU storage or uninstalling ESPU deletes this AppSearch data.

If you explicitly ask ESPU AI to call one exact person or ordinary phone number, ESPU first shows the exact contact and normalized number in a visible per-call review. Choosing Cancel or dismissing that review opens no call or dialer. Choosing Call may then trigger ESPU's separate just-in-time explanation and Android's Phone-permission prompt. Carrier/USSD strings and pause/wait dialing codes are rejected. Emergency numbers, an uncertain platform emergency verdict, uncertain modem routing, or a missing default-Phone role use the protected ACTION_DIAL surface so Android's dialer retains final confirmation and emergency-routing authority. An ordinary direct call can be placed only after Android verifies that ESPU holds the default-Phone role and grants CALL_PHONE; ESPU then asks Android Telecom to place that validated number through TelecomManager.placeCall. Permission denial or cancelling the permission explanation opens nothing. ESPU does not call from search results, suggestions, recommendations, or ambiguous contacts, and this direct-call path does not read or write call history.

If you separately choose ESPU as Android's default Phone app, Android Telecom provides ESPU with the active call state, caller display information, supported call capabilities, and available audio endpoints needed to present incoming and ongoing call controls. Verified ESPU Island controls can include Answer, Decline, End, Mute, Android-provided audio routes such as available Bluetooth devices, Keypad, and Hold when the active call supports it. If another Phone app remains selected, ESPU disables incoming and ongoing call cards and call actions instead of deriving call ownership from that app's notification; ordinary notification, message, media, voicemail, and compatible live-activity Island surfaces remain available. Android Telecom and system safeguards retain protected emergency-call routing.

While ESPU remains the default Phone app, you may separately grant read-only Call history and Contacts permissions to show Recents and local contacts inside ESPU Phone and start a call you select. ESPU stops querying these sources when it is no longer the default Phone app or the permission is revoked. If you long-press one recent call or choose Clear all, ESPU shows a confirmation first. Only after confirmation, while ESPU is still the default Phone app, may ESPU request Android's Call history change permission and delete the selected record or all records from Android's on-device call history. ESPU does not otherwise edit call history. Call history and contacts are processed on your device. ESPU does not record or upload call audio, create a cloud call history, upload contacts, or use call information for advertising or analytics.

Today may show a bounded missed-call follow-up view under the same default-Phone and read-only Call history gates. Rows are read from Android on demand and are not copied into an ESPU call-history database. Callback fills the dialer and never places a call automatically. Reminder and post-call-note content is stored only after you select that action. Local caller warnings are not a network reputation service and never claim a caller is certainly fraudulent. A Trusted or Possible-scam choice stores only an app-keyed HMAC token, disposition, and update time—not the number or name—and never blocks or rejects a call.

If you choose a voicemail audio file through Android's document picker, ESPU can transcribe a supported mono 16-bit PCM WAV locally with the integrity-verified Vosk model. The selected audio, transcript, and extractive summary remain in process memory unless you explicitly save them as an ESPU Note. ESPU does not record cellular calls, read carrier voicemail automatically, retain the selected audio file, or upload its contents. Unsupported files and an unavailable verified model produce an unavailable state rather than a remote fallback.

ESPU does not request Phone or Phone state access during first-run setup. If you open ESPU Phone and ask to choose an outgoing SIM or eSIM, ESPU first shows a separate explanation and may then request Android's read-only Phone state permission. That access is used only to list Android-provided enabled call-capable accounts and route your outgoing call through the account you select. ESPU does not read or store your phone number, SIM serial, ICCID, call content, or a raw Android account handle. If you decline, Android can still choose the calling account. A one-way local digest may remember the selection for matching only on your device and is cleared when the account disappears. ESPU does not request ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS. Answer, Decline, Mute, audio route, Keypad, Hold, and End use Android Telecom only while ESPU holds the default-Phone role.

If you enable screen-lock access, Android grants ESPU only the device-admin force-lock policy. ESPU invokes it only after your double-tap gesture or an ESPU AI voice command that you confirm on screen. ESPU does not erase the device, change its screen-lock credential, restart or power off the device, or administer other policies.

Private Space uses Android's system authentication prompt. ESPU does not receive or store fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or password data. Hidden choices affect ESPU's interface; they do not uninstall or encrypt an app.

ESPU does not request hidden-profile access and does not expose or control Android's system Private Space. If Android nevertheless reports an app as belonging to a private profile, ESPU excludes it from ordinary All Applications. This is separate from ESPU's own hidden-app Private Space described above.

Battery/charging status, power-saver state, local time zone, and Android wallpaper colors may be read locally to adapt the interface. ESPU does not request location access.

Focus Hub offers optional Android Usage Access. Only after you explicitly grant this special access in Android Settings does ESPU read package name, foreground duration, and last-used time for a current-day, on-device screen-time summary. The raw usage data has no network path and is not persisted. ESPU stores only one non-identifying prior-grant bit in app-private preferences so it can distinguish access that was never granted from access that was later revoked; clearing ESPU storage or uninstalling ESPU deletes that bit.

ESPU AI processes typing and explicit voice requests locally through its compact Action Model and bounded deterministic command handlers. Each supported action still uses the same permission, role, confirmation, current-state, and result checks at its execution boundary. ESPU can open installed apps and supported Android destinations, start timers, open alarms and calendar views, prepare communication or calendar drafts for review, control an active media session, answer limited device-status questions, and change supported ESPU launcher settings. “Deep Thinking” is deterministic on-device planning and comparison logic; it is not a remote generative-AI service and does not claim live facts.

ESPU AI includes ESPU's own compact Action Model in the signed app. Its signed base is immutable at runtime. It recognizes supported requests and proposes typed actions, but it has no Android privileges. Reviewed code still enforces device capability, profile privacy, roles, permissions, confirmations, and result checks. It is an action model, not an open-ended language model, and it does not claim cached facts are current.

Optional automatic action learning is off until you enable it. When enabled, a bounded local action-matching adapter learns hashed feature weights only from verified low-risk outcomes; it does not change the signed Action Model base. You may separately teach a private phrase only by linking it to one supported low-risk open action; ambiguous, sensitive, external, contact, message, search, and navigation examples are rejected. ESPU does not keep raw commands, contact names, phone numbers, message text, or document contents as action-model training examples. You can disable, inspect, forget, reset, or erase that adapter. Android Clear storage or a full uninstall removes it. Apart from the explicit selected-file OCR described here, no remote vision, general background screen reader, cross-device, app-skill, enterprise, or OEM provider is active in ESPU. Any later provider would require separate signed delivery, review, compatibility, consent, and real device authority.

If you explicitly select a JPEG, PNG, WebP, or PDF through Android's document picker, ESPU can run bundled Latin-script OCR on that content. Images are bounded and PDFs are limited to the first three pages. ESPU persists only the selected content URI and basic searchable attachment metadata; recognized text and receipt hints remain in process memory unless you explicitly save them as a Note. Forget releases the persisted Android read grant. ESPU does not request broad photo/file access, capture screenshots, operate an Accessibility service, scrape another app, or upload the selected content. Google states that ML Kit processes feature inputs and outputs on-device. Its SDK may transmit encrypted diagnostics and usage measurements such as app/device information, a non-user-identifying per-installation identifier, performance, API configuration, input/output sizes, feature version, event type, and error codes; Google states those measurements are not shared with third parties.

Suggested Apps & Coach learning is a separate opt-in control and on-device adapter; it does not modify the signed Action Model or its action-matching adapter. Each bounded, profile-aware app or allow-listed action target uses its own 20 → 8 → 4 → 1 multilayer perceptron with two hidden layers. Its score is advisory and can only rerank suggestions already admitted by ESPU's existing contextual or Coach rules. Everyday Apps remains the default Home dock, and manual ordering or an explicit customer choice always wins.

The private neural store can contain bounded profile-aware app/action identifiers, counters and coarse context metadata, per-target weights and biases, and at most 48 anonymous training timestamps used only to enforce the strict trailing-24-hour quota. The quota timestamps have no target or content association and are not a raw app-activity timeline. The store does not retain raw commands, note/document/message/contact content, or a copy of the installed-app catalog. Training occurs only when Private learning is enabled and ESPU receives a verified low-risk outcome or explicit suggestion feedback. ESPU skips optional training under app-lifecycle, battery/power, thermal, or memory pressure and accepts no more than 48 updates in a trailing 24-hour window. There is no timer, background training service, network upload, or cloud-model synchronization. You can delete one exact app/action target and its counters/model or globally reset private neural prediction. Turning learning off stops new training and hides learned suggestions.

ESPU 2.3 is Suggestions-only. Neural output cannot open an app, change a setting, execute a routine, create a grant or permission, or call an executor. Scheduler cues remain not-run Review & Run presentations. Suggested Apps remains unavailable until ESPU records at least 3 successful launcher opens across at least 2 local days, and appears only after you explicitly select Suggested. ESPU never opens the suggested app by itself, and Coach setting changes require your approval. You can inspect or reset learned and taught matching signals. While Private Space is locked, ESPU AI filters hidden apps from notification results.

ESPU Notes stores plain notes, checklists, pin choices, and the compatible Quick Note in app-private local storage. ESPU has no email-account connector or automatic mailbox sync. If you explicitly choose Android's Share or Process Text action and select ESPU, ESPU stores only that selected payload as a local Intent Inbox draft. Manual drafts and notification content explicitly saved for follow-up use the same bounded local store. You can review or delete each draft. Converting a draft to a note happens locally; reminder, calendar, call, or message actions open an Android-owned review surface and are not treated as complete merely because that surface opened. ESPU does not continuously read another app or mailbox.

The optional Calendar Agenda is off by default and asks for Android's read-only Calendar permission only after you enable it in Today. Each read covers at most the next 36 hours and 24 items. ESPU uses title, time, all-day state, location, and calendar label for the visible agenda and opt-in local Search; Daily Brief receives only an item count and next-event time. Results are reused in process memory for at most 60 seconds and are cleared when you turn the feature off or the app process ends. ESPU does not persist agenda records, query attendees, account addresses, organizers, or event notes, or request Calendar write permission for this feature.

Personal Archive can create a passphrase-encrypted copy of ESPU Notes, today's priorities, validated routines, AI privacy choices, and Island preferences in a folder you choose through Android's Storage Access Framework. AES-256-GCM encryption happens before Android writes the file to the selected provider. ESPU does not save or recover your passphrase, has no provider account integration, and performs no automatic or background archive sync. Preview, per-section conflict choices, export, import, encrypted-copy deletion, folder-access release, and erasing the covered on-phone data are explicit foreground actions. The selected provider can observe file metadata and may transport the encrypted file under its own terms.

ESPU is an Android Home app with an optional default-Phone mode, not an operating system. Android owns protected system UI, emergency routing, car-display and Android Auto surfaces, permission prompts, package installation, and package removal. ESPU does not have unrestricted access to your phone. Android roles, permissions, installed-app support, confirmation screens, and protected-system limits determine which actions can run. ESPU does not bypass Android security, silently send messages, place unsolicited calls, save calendar entries without review, install apps, change protected settings, or read another app's private data. A direct call occurs only after an explicit call command, exact-target validation, a visible per-call target review, the in-context permission disclosure when needed, verified ESPU default-Phone role ownership, and Android's CALL_PHONE permission.

ESPU AI does not access, read, or store passwords, PINs, banking details, or payment credentials. Never enter them in an ESPU AI command.

Voice is tap-to-talk. ESPU accesses the microphone only after you tap Voice and does not keep a background wake listener running. ESPU prefers on-device recognition and includes an offline Vosk fallback in the applicable build. Depending on device support and configuration, Android's configured speech provider may process that spoken command under the provider's privacy terms. ESPU has no ESPU voice-upload server.

Home weather ambience can use the device clock for automatic scenes. If you enable live weather, ESPU stores the selected place, resolved coordinates, and latest conditions in private local storage so the ambience can be restored. In this closed-testing candidate, ESPU sends the city or postal code you enter directly over HTTPS to Open-Meteo's geocoding service, then sends the resolved latitude and longitude directly to Open-Meteo's forecast service. ESPU does not request Android device-location permission for this evaluation feature. Turning off live sync clears the saved live-weather profile. Production live weather remains blocked until an ESPU-controlled HTTPS proxy origin and its exact logging, retention, IP-address handling, and subprocessors are reviewed and approved; this closed-testing statement does not finalize that configuration.

If you choose My Photo, Android's system document picker grants ESPU persistent read access only to the image you selected. ESPU stores the selected content reference and a locally sampled average color, then uses Android's WallpaperManager to apply the image to the system Home wallpaper and, when you explicitly choose it on a supported device, the Lock wallpaper. ESPU does not request gallery or broad file access or upload the photo. Android Auto and other car-display backgrounds remain independently controlled by Android and the vehicle.

ESPU listens for Android package-added and package-removed events so newly installed launcher apps can appear without a manual reload and confirmed removals can disappear. Choosing Uninstall opens Android's required confirmation screen; ESPU refreshes its local launcher data only after Android confirms the package is gone.

Android widgets remain provided by their source apps. ESPU shows one compatible widget choice per visible installed app and stores Android-assigned widget identifiers locally so it can host up to four independent Home widget cards. Removing a widget releases its identifier but does not uninstall its provider app.

Sharing and backup

ESPU does not automatically share personal data. If you choose Export Backup, ESPU creates a JSON file of launcher preferences and hands it to Android's system share/document interface. You choose the destination. Backup files can contain app package names and hidden/focus choices.

Personal Archive is separate from that readable backup. It manages only the encrypted ESPU-Personal-Archive.espu file in a folder you explicitly select. ESPU writes no plaintext archive payload through this path.

Retention and deletion

Launcher data remains on the device until you change it, restore a backup, clear ESPU storage, or uninstall ESPU. Notes and Intent Inbox drafts remain until you delete them, clear ESPU storage, or uninstall ESPU. An encrypted Personal Archive remains in your selected folder until you delete that named copy there or through ESPU; forgetting the folder releases ESPU's access but does not delete the file. ESPU never retains the archive passphrase. ESPU AI settings let you inspect or forget Action Model action-learning signals and reset that adapter. Private neural controls separately delete one exact app/action target or globally reset neural prediction. A focused erase removes all ESPU AI-owned adapter and suggestion state without removing your launcher layout or apps. Notification content is not copied by default; a draft copy exists only after Save for follow-up. Revoking Notification Access stops ESPU from reading active notifications but does not silently delete drafts you previously chose to save. ESPU does not create a user account.

Network access

Direct device commands, local ideas, Action Model learning, and private neural prediction have no ESPU AI-server or remote generative-model integration. The private neural adapter performs training and inference on the device and has no network or cloud synchronization path. A configured Android speech provider may process spoken commands as described above. ESPU does not send learned app patterns, model weights/biases, installed apps, contacts, notifications, Home layout, launcher data, or command history to an AI provider. The selected-file OCR SDK diagnostics described above can include app/device information and a non-user-identifying per-installation identifier, but not the selected image, recognized text, or recognition output. The ESPU-owned Action Model is part of the signed app; optional future capability packs remain unavailable until separately signed, reviewed, consented, and tested.

This candidate uses Google Play Asset Delivery: the wallpaper collection is fast-follow, while Theme Packs and offline voice assets are on-demand. To deliver and preserve these packs, Google Play collects device metadata and application version information. Google states that this information is encrypted, is not transferred to third parties, and is deleted after a fixed retention period. The built-in ESPU Action Model does not require a separately downloaded model archive.

When you manually export or apply a reviewed Personal Archive sync, Android may give the encrypted archive file to the local or cloud-backed document provider you selected. ESPU receives no provider account token and gives that provider no plaintext archive data. ESPU does not schedule later transfers; the provider independently handles any transport.
