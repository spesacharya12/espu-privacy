ESPU AI Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Applies to: ESPU AI Launcher 1.2 and later until this policy is updated.

ESPU AI Launcher operates on your Android device without an ESPU account. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, subscription system, or remote customer-data service.

Data used on the device

ESPU reads apps that expose launcher activities so it can work as a home-screen replacement. It can also read publisher-declared launcher shortcuts for an installed app when you ask to see or run them. It stores launcher preferences—such as your optional Home greeting name, favorites, recents, hidden choices, focus choices, freeform positions, themes, and quick notes—in the app's private local storage.

If you explicitly grant Notification Access, ESPU can display active notification titles and message previews in the ESPU Island and in the relevant app's long-press preview. ESPU can offer a reply when the source app provides Android's standard quick-reply action. ESPU processes this information on the device, does not create a separate notification inbox, and does not upload or persist notification content. If you snooze an app's ESPU notification presentation, ESPU stores only that app's package name and the snooze expiry time. Repeating charging, USB, battery, System UI, and persistent service notices are filtered from ESPU surfaces and remain Android's responsibility.

If you explicitly grant Contacts access and ask ESPU AI to call or text a saved person, ESPU searches contact names and phone numbers on the device to prepare the requested Android dialer or message draft. ESPU does not persist or upload contact records.

If you enable screen-lock access, Android grants ESPU only the device-admin force-lock policy. ESPU invokes it only after your double-tap gesture or an ESPU AI voice command that you confirm on screen. ESPU does not erase the device, change its screen-lock credential, restart or power off the device, or administer other policies.

Private Space uses Android's system authentication prompt. ESPU does not receive or store fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or password data. Hidden choices affect ESPU's interface; they do not uninstall or encrypt an app.

Battery/charging status, power-saver state, local time zone, Android wallpaper colors, and an optional low-rate ambient-light band may be read locally to adapt the interface. Raw light-sensor samples are not stored, uploaded, or used for profiling. Phones without a light sensor keep the normal presentation. ESPU does not request location access.

ESPU AI uses one local command path for typing, explicit voice requests, and Home recommendations. It can open installed apps, publisher-declared app shortcuts, and supported Android destinations; start timers; open alarms and calendar views; prepare communication or calendar drafts for review; control an active media session; answer limited device-status, time, and configured-weather questions; and change ESPU launcher settings. Customer-named routines and preferred-app choices are stored locally and remain customer-controlled. “Deep Thinking” is deterministic on-device planning and comparison logic; it is not a remote generative-AI service and does not claim live facts.

If you explicitly send selected text to ESPU through Android's share interface, ESPU can process that text locally to summarize it, make a note, or prepare another customer-requested action. Choosing copy, web search, navigation, messaging, email, or another destination hands only the information needed for that action to Android or the destination app you choose, which operates under its own privacy terms.

When you enable Private adaptive learning, ESPU AI stores bounded app-launch counters, weekday/weekend time contexts, recent-use timestamps, next-app counters, and suggestion feedback in private local storage. Turning it off pauses new habit collection. ESPU does not retain a raw activity timeline. App-habit suggestions require repeated, recent on-device evidence and ESPU asks before applying every change. While Private Space is locked, ESPU AI filters hidden apps from notification results.

ESPU is an Android Home app, not an operating system. Android owns protected system UI, car-display and Android Auto surfaces, permission prompts, package installation, and package removal. ESPU does not have unrestricted access to your phone. Android permissions, installed-app support, confirmation screens, and protected-system limits determine which actions can run. ESPU does not bypass Android security, silently send messages, place calls, save calendar entries, install apps, change protected settings, or read another app's private data.

ESPU AI does not access, read, or store passwords, PINs, banking details, or payment credentials. Never enter them in an ESPU AI command.

If you enable “Hey Espu” anywhere, ESPU uses microphone access in a user-visible Android foreground service. ESPU prefers on-device recognition and includes a bundled offline Vosk fallback. Depending on the device and Android speech-provider configuration, a command may be processed by the configured Android speech provider under that provider's privacy terms. ESPU does not send voice recordings to an ESPU server. Android shows a persistent microphone notification and you can turn listening off at any time.

Home weather ambience can use the device clock for automatic scenes or retrieve weather for a place you explicitly enter. A live-weather request sends that place query, and the coordinates resolved from it, to the weather/geocoding service over HTTPS. ESPU does not use Android device-location permission.

If you choose My Photo for wallpaper, Android's system document picker grants ESPU persistent read access only to the image you selected. ESPU stores the selected content reference and a locally sampled average color, then uses Android's WallpaperManager to apply the image to the system Home wallpaper and, when you explicitly choose it on a supported device, the Lock wallpaper.

If you choose Special Moments for the personal Home card, Android's system photo picker lets you select up to five images. ESPU stores only those selected content references and locally sampled colors so the card can rotate the photos on the device. The references are removed when you clear the card. ESPU does not request broad gallery or file access, upload these photos, or include them in ESPU backup files. Android Auto and other car-display backgrounds remain independently controlled by Android and the vehicle.

ESPU listens for Android package-added and package-removed events so newly installed launcher apps can appear without a manual reload and confirmed removals can disappear. Choosing Uninstall opens Android's required confirmation screen; ESPU refreshes its local launcher data only after Android confirms the package is gone.

Android widgets remain provided by their source apps. ESPU shows one compatible widget choice per visible installed app and stores Android-assigned widget identifiers locally so it can host five Home widget slots and, if you choose it instead of Special Moments, one compatible app widget in the personal Home card. Widget content and configuration remain controlled by the provider app. Removing a widget releases its identifier but does not uninstall its provider app.

Launcher Health stores aggregate reliability counts, limited status values, and timestamps for ESPU feature areas so it can show current readiness and a private seven-day outlook. It does not store raw error text or personal content, and it is not a medical or whole-phone diagnosis. This health information remains on the device and is not automatically reported to ESPU.

To avoid repetitive customer-care messages, ESPU locally stores release acknowledgement, first-welcome time, optional anniversary-card dismissal, and Google Play update-snooze time. These values are not analytics or an engagement profile.

Sharing and backup

ESPU does not automatically share personal data. If you choose Export Backup, ESPU creates a JSON file and hands it to Android's system share/document interface. You choose the destination. The backup uses an explicit allowlist: launcher settings, theme schedule, favorites, custom app order, focus choices, freeform layout, customer-named routines, and preferred-app choices. These fields can contain app package names.

ESPU backups exclude photos and photo references, Quick Note, daily plans, hidden and recent apps, notifications, contacts, voice data, weather place, greeting name, Android permissions, widget identifiers, and learned habits.

If you choose Private support snapshot, ESPU prepares a text summary containing the app and Android versions, feature-readiness states, voice-enabled state, grid dimensions, drawer mode, theme identifier, and selected performance/accessibility flags. It excludes personal names, app inventory, notification content, commands, contacts, location, device identifiers, Quick Note, and learned habits. Nothing is sent until you choose a destination in Android's share panel.

Retention and deletion

Launcher data remains on the device until you change or clear the relevant feature, restore a backup, clear ESPU storage, or uninstall ESPU. ESPU AI settings let you inspect aggregate learning totals and erase learned habits and Coach feedback without resetting your launcher layout or notes. Saved routines and preferred-app choices can be individually changed or removed. Launcher Health history can be cleared from its controls. Revoking Notification Access stops ESPU from reading active notifications. ESPU does not create a user account, so no separate account-deletion request is required.

Network access

Direct device commands, local wake-word recognition, local ideas, routines, Launcher Health, and adaptive learning have no ESPU AI-server or remote AI-provider integration. ESPU does not send learned app patterns, installed apps, contacts, notifications, Home layout, launcher data, or a device identifier to an AI provider.

Network access is limited to features that require a network destination: weather/geocoding for a place you enter; the device's configured Android speech provider when it is used; Android or destination apps for customer-requested web, navigation, communication, sharing, or similar handoffs; and Google Play services for update checks and delivery in Google Play builds. Google Play's official in-app update interface may return update availability, version code, staleness, priority, and download state. Google Play controls customer consent, download, verification, installation, and restart. Standalone builds do not claim Google Play update availability.

No sale or advertising use

ESPU does not sell personal data, share personal data for targeted advertising, or include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
